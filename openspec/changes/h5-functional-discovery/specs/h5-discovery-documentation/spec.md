## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Traceable H5 functional extension

The documentation SHALL develop H5's existing combined-stage, live, email and ecosystem scope with concrete rules, incidents, complete individual/direct-team journeys, observable target acceptance and owned unresolved gates, distinguishing confirmed scope, proposed defaults and implementation evidence.

#### Scenario: Reader evaluates target rules

- **WHEN** a reader follows H5 from the baseline or README
- **THEN** the H5 extension explains stage/cut authority, tie/seed examples, current-source barriers, recovery, delivery and access lifecycles without claiming policy approval or prerequisite completion

### Requirement: One maintained source per responsibility

The documentation MUST retain decision status solely in baseline §15, detailed H5 rules in its extension, delivery sequence in the roadmap and future screen/state coverage in visual direction, with consistent source conventions and links.

#### Scenario: Reader locates a pending policy and its delivery gate

- **WHEN** a reader examines an H5 recommendation
- **THEN** its available decision identifier links to baseline §15 and its responsible reviewer/implementation gate is explicit, while delivery and visual references point to their maintained sources

### Requirement: Documentation-only validation and handoff

The change SHALL remain documentary, record actual validation and independent review evidence, preserve implemented specifications and the published-event inventory, and describe acceptance scenarios as future targets.

#### Scenario: Reviewer checks readiness claims

- **WHEN** the reviewer examines this change and its validation record
- **THEN** documentation checks and resolved independent findings are distinguished from H5 acceptance not executed, with no production code, runtime generation, published-event additions or H5 targets moved to implemented specifications
