## Decisions

1. Functional detail belongs in the H5 extension; decision status in baseline §15 D-57–65 (next available at base); delivery in roadmap; future visual inventory in visual direction. The delta specifies documentation requirements rather than runtime H5 scenarios, avoiding a second behavioural authority and accidental promotion to implemented specs.
2. Develop a bounded two-template first scope rather than an arbitrary stage graph. Use fixed quotas and group crossings, explicit shared-rank/selection/seed distinctions and an independently reproducible terminal-selector fixture. A generic graph editor or cross-group raw-score comparison would hide unresolved sporting rules. All candidate defaults remain proposed, including the more conservative published-dependency correction blocker inherited from H3/H4.
3. Preserve current-source cut and publication barriers across owning modules. Tournaments owns plan/lifecycle/visibility; Registration eligibility; Competition stage/cut/pairing/result transitions; Standings applied rankings; Notifications email/preferences under target ADR 0017. Future technical designs establish authoritative revision checks, durable transition outcomes and cancellation ordering without module references, cross-schema reads or feature-to-feature calls. No storage schema, module or integration contract changes here. Public webhook ownership/security remains a future design gate, not automatically assigned to Notifications.
4. Distinguish live invalidation/snapshot convergence, optional human email and durable server webhook acknowledgement. Reusing one delivery contract would falsely promise ordered browser events, exactly-once external effects or proof of human reading. Provide explicit snapshot/cursor barriers, bounded retry/replay candidates, unknown email acceptance and in-flight revocation limits, with real-consumer/provider evidence gates.
5. Preserve alias-only public data, individual simplicity and stable direct-team entries/rosters. A cut is not registration, transfer or new competitive identity. Detailed operational/security recommendations remain product/technical review gates; no general opening, provider choice, credential creation or official sporting-rule compatibility is approved.
6. Documentation-only proposal: no screen is created/modified; mockups and executable UI scripts are not applicable. Future surfaces/states are linked from H5 §14 to the canonical inventory, including stale/denied/uncertain-save, mobile and accessibility. These remain pending visual review under `herald-screens` and ADR 0018 before implementation readiness.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Detailed numerical examples may look approved → register/proposal/extension explicitly label recommendations and require owner decisions before the affected implementation/release gate.
- Cut correction can leave stale published opponents → current-input barriers, invalidated applicability and conservative dependency blocker, with acceptance for the publication race.
- Delivery guarantees may exceed external control → separate provider accepted/delivered/read, logical/attempt identity and current-authority suppression; disclose downloaded/in-flight limitations.
- Candidate limits may be taken as a service promise → D-65 requires measured caps, retention, support/cost and approval before release; no unlimited default where a value is unresolved.
- Main may advance during review → refresh main and check source/register/anchor overlap and exact scope before handoff; retain unrelated progress without rewriting historical evidence.
