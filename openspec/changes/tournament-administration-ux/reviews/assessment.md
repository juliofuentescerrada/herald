## Preserve during remediation

- Full-width one-column admin composition, square geometry, shared spacing/tokens, single 700 px breakpoint, status beside record title except the documented fixed-title review page.
- Links navigate; buttons execute. One primary action per operation area; independent operations may each have a primary.
- Draft saving before completeness; saved review before publication; explicit registration, schedule publication and competition start; no automatic transition from dates.
- Distinct admission/check-in/roster/lineup readiness; initial results unselected; individual result may start the match, team played result requires locked lineups.
- Read-only result after resolution; retry identity/revisions, explicit conflict recovery and server-authoritative success. No corrections or new result causes inferred from UX aspirations.
- Private-workspace replacement on loss of authority; no cached records from another organisation. Duplicate guests remain explicit, not merged.
- Cancellation/absence consequences and private reasons; local errors; cancelled roster inspection; no-toast roster edits. No added confirmation for every reversible choice.

## Candidate scope to discuss before a proposal

1. **Shared tournament context and navigation:** list access, lifecycle presentation, section movement, phase-aware information hierarchy. Includes configuration, entries, schedule and match headers; publication review retains its specific composition.
2. **Preparation and entries:** creation/editing comprehension, publication recovery, registration/start clarity, guest-search retry and action emphasis, roster action grouping/history. Layout refinements must preserve current policy and permissions.
3. **Competition operation:** schedule match rows and clear destinations/results; match outcome selection and saved summary; priority of results versus locked lineups; consistent conflict/retry/discard handling.
4. **Cross-cutting acceptance:** responsive/long-content checks, keyboard/focus, local feedback, numeric roles and only the supporting shared components/catalogue examples actually affected.

These groups are assessment scope, not four approved slices or a commitment to change every screen. Creation and publication have source-positive foundations; their remaining browser review can result in no visual change. A shell redesign, wizard, new dashboard, global search/filter, bulk operations, calendar export and new role system are not implied.

Current active `h1-exceptional-outcomes`, `h1-round-operations` and `h1-standings` changes touch the same competition surfaces. Coordinate against the branch chosen for implementation: their current local approval markers do not establish approval elsewhere. Do not rewrite those approvals or implement their deltas as visual fixes. Standings, departures/sanctions, corrections, finalisation, cancellation/archive and H2 public/participant/reporting flows are future/dependent scope on this checkout, not missing current screens to add here. H2 design targets remain separate under XP-02–08 and D-30–38.
