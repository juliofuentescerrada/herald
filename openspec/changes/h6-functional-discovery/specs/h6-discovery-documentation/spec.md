## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Traceable H6 functional extension

The documentation SHALL develop H6's existing advanced-team and game-submission scope with concrete profiles, exact aggregation, participation changes, incidents, protected submissions, complete journeys and observable target acceptance while distinguishing accepted scope, proposed defaults and implemented behaviour.

#### Scenario: Reader assesses a proposed H6 journey

- **WHEN** a reader follows H6 from the baseline or README
- **THEN** the extension explains composite, direct-team and individual boundaries, correction/recovery and privacy without claiming policy approval or prerequisite completion

### Requirement: One source per responsibility

The documentation MUST keep decision status in baseline §15, H6 functional detail in its extension, delivery sequence in roadmap and future surface/state inventory in visual direction, with consistent orientation and contributor conventions.

#### Scenario: Reader locates an unresolved recommendation

- **WHEN** a reader examines an H6 recommendation
- **THEN** it has an available register identifier, responsible reviewer and implementation/release gate, with links to maintained delivery and visual sources rather than a competing status register

### Requirement: Documentary validation and handoff

The change SHALL remain documentary, preserve implemented specifications and the published-event inventory, and record actual checks and independent review separately from future product acceptance.

#### Scenario: Reviewer checks evidence and scope

- **WHEN** the reviewer examines the diff and validation record
- **THEN** there are no runtime changes or H6 targets promoted into implemented specifications, and completed documentation checks/findings are distinguishable from product scenarios not executed
