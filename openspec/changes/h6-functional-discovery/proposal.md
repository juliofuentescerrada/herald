## Why

H6's existing scope promises composite encounters, substitutions, exceptional roster amendments and private game submissions without sufficient aggregation, timing, authority or recovery rules. This authorised documentation discovery makes those choices concrete for review without approving policies or implementing them.

## What Changes

- Add `docs/herald-h6-functional-discovery.md` with bounded profiles, exact board aggregation, participation revisions, substitution/amendment windows, private submission lifecycle, incidents, complete journeys and observable target acceptance.
- Add proposed D-66–74 to baseline §15, the next available identifiers; keep detailed rules in the extension, delivery sequence in roadmap and future visual inventory in visual direction.
- Update orientation/source conventions following H2/H3 and verify prerequisites from current implemented specifications, without claiming H1–H5 completion.

Decision dependencies: D-16 and accepted ADR 0013 establish scope/sequence; D-19 confirms exclusion of in-match substitutions from H1, not an H6 policy. D-08 ownership and IP-01–06 individual simplicity remain constraints; D-09/17/18 are partial and D-07/23/24/34/37/38, H3/H4 profile/authority gates and H5 D-57–65 retain their recorded proposed detail. New D-66–74 remain recommendations for maintainer review. No product policy is approved by this documentary commission.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `h6-discovery-documentation`: maintain the H6 functional extension, single-source links and documentation-only handoff.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Implemented requirements in `openspec/specs/` remain unchanged.

## Impact

Documentation only: no production code, endpoint, dependency, database, infrastructure or credential change. No screen is created or modified, so mockups and executable screen scripts are not applicable. The future surface/state inventory remains subject to review in later slices. No runtime notifiable fact is introduced; candidate notices do not change ADR 0017's recorded/published inventory. No Wolverine/OpenAPI generation, product test execution or implementation approval is implied.
